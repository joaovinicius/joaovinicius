Great software is the result of deep collaboration. Here are the types of challenges I partner with teams to solve:

- **Complex SaaS & Multi-Tenant Platforms:** Architecting systems with strict data isolation, configurable workflows, and high reliability.
- **Payment & Transactional Pipelines:** Integrating robust payment gateways, automated Pix flows, webhooks, and consistent billing lifecycles.

- **Specialized Line-of-Business Tools:** Creating tailored software for unique industry operations, legal intake, healthcare, and offline-first workflows.

### 💡 How I Like to Work With Teams

1. **Understand Before Building:** We look closely at the real human workflow, the business constraints, and the user's daily friction before touching code.
2. **Predictable & Maintainable:** We use explicit contracts (TypeScript, OpenAPI), clean domain models, and automated tests to make sure software behaves as expected and is easy for everyone on the team to maintain.
3. **Tools That Help People:** Technology should reduce stress, eliminate manual toil, and empower users to do their best work.
